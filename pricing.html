Welfare / Funeral Scheme

Our Welfare/Funeral Scheme provides financial support to members and their families in times of bereavement. The scheme covers funeral expenses for the principal member, spouse, and up to 3 children, ensuring peace of mind and dignity for your loved ones.

  • → Eligibility: All active members in good standing
  • → Coverage: Principal member, spouse, up to 3 children
  • → Benefit: Lump sum funeral payout (see office for current amount)
  • → Premium: Affordable monthly deduction from savings

For full terms, benefit amounts, and claims process, please contact the SACCOS office.
